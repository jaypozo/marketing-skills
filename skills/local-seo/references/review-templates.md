# Review requests and responses

Adapt these to the business's voice. Keep requests short and the link direct. Send them to every customer, without screening first. Healthcare, legal and financial businesses use the privacy-safe replies near the end.

## Request, by text, right after the job or visit

```text
Hi [first name], thanks for choosing [Business] today. If you have a minute, a review helps other people in [town] find us: [direct review link]. [Name], [Business]
```

## Request, by email, same day

```text
Subject: How did we do today?

Hi [first name],

Thanks for choosing [Business] for [service] today.

Would you leave a short review? It takes about a minute, and it helps people nearby decide who to call.

[Leave a review]

If anything wasn't right, reply to this email and it comes straight to me.

[Owner name]
```

The last line gives unhappy customers a direct route to the owner. It must not replace the review link or be sent instead of it. Everyone gets the same email.

## Reminder, three to five days later (once only)

```text
Hi [first name], just a reminder in case it got buried: [direct review link]. Thanks again. [Business]
```

## Replying to a positive review

Use the person's first name. Mention something specific from the review. Thank them, in one or two sentences.

```text
Thanks, Maria. Glad the new thermostat is keeping the upstairs warm. Kevin says hi.
```

Don't paste the same reply under every review.

## Replying to a negative review

1. Thank them and acknowledge the specific problem. Don't argue the details in public.
2. Say what you'll do, or what you've already done.
3. Give a named person and a direct contact.
4. Keep it under about 80 words.

```text
Paul, I'm sorry the technician arrived two hours after the window we gave you. That's not acceptable, and I've changed how we schedule afternoon calls. I'd like to make it right. Please call me directly on [number]. Sam, owner
```

If the review is fake (no record of the customer) or breaks the platform's rules, report it through the platform. Reply briefly and politely in the meantime.

## Privacy-safe replies (healthcare, legal, financial)

Never confirm that the reviewer is a patient or client, and never mention a visit, treatment, case or account, even if they did first. Replies stay general.

Positive:

```text
Thank you for taking the time to leave a review. We're glad to hear it, and we'll pass your kind words on to the team.
```

Negative:

```text
Thank you for the feedback. We can't discuss anyone's care in a public reply. Please call our practice manager, [name], on [number], and we'll look into it.
```

Texting reminders and review requests to patients needs their consent first. Any tool that sends them on your behalf needs a signed business associate agreement in US healthcare.

## Things never to do

- Ask only customers you expect to be happy, or check satisfaction first and send the link only to happy ones.
- Offer discounts, gifts or entry to a draw in exchange for reviews.
- Write reviews for the business, or have staff, family or agencies write them.
- Set up a shared tablet or kiosk at the business for customers to leave reviews on. Google's policy bans review stations. A printed QR code customers scan on their own phone is fine.
