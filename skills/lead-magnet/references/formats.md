# Lead magnet formats

Build times assume one person who knows the subject. Pick the cheapest format that gets the reader past the obstacle.

| Format | Best when | Build time | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Printable checklist | The task happens away from the screen, or has steps people forget | 1 to 3 hours | Lists of obvious steps. Every line should save a mistake. |
| Fill-in template | The reader has to write or set up something (contract, brief, email, menu, policy) | 2 to 4 hours | Templates so generic they need rewriting from scratch |
| Swipe file | The reader wants examples to copy and adapt | 3 to 6 hours | Copyright. Use your own examples or ones you have permission to share. |
| Spreadsheet or calculator | The answer depends on the reader's own numbers (pricing, budget, dosage, yield) | 3 to 8 hours | Formulas nobody checked. Test with three realistic cases. |
| Comparison table or scorecard | The reader is choosing between options | 2 to 5 hours | Hiding your own product's weak spots. Readers who know the market spot it and trust the rest less. |
| 5-day email course | The change takes practice, or the reader needs reminding | 4 to 8 hours | Days that pad. Each email needs one task of 15 minutes or less. |
| Recorded walkthrough (5 to 15 min) | The reader needs to see it done | 3 to 6 hours | Long intros. Start with the result. |
| Resource list | The reader needs to find suppliers, tools or services | 1 to 3 hours | Lists that go out of date. Put a "last checked" date on it. |
| Quiz with a tailored result | Readers fall into a few clearly different situations | 6 to 12 hours | Results that all say the same thing |

## Naming

Name it with the outcome and the format. "The 7-day sourdough starter feeding schedule" is better than "Free sourdough guide". So is "Freelance rate calculator: find your day rate in 3 minutes" compared with "Pricing toolkit".

## Delivery

- Host files where the link won't break when the site is rebuilt.
- PDFs: under about 5 MB, readable on a phone, the shop or site address on every page.
- Spreadsheets: share as "make a copy", or give a download, so readers don't edit the master.
- Email courses: send day 1 straight away (with double opt-in, straight after confirmation), then one a day. Say in day 1 how many are coming.
